How validation works

  • Each photo is sent to an open vision model (Llama Vision via Hugging Face), which returns merchant, date, and total as JSON.
  • The backend validates that JSON before it reaches the table.
  • A photo the model does not confirm as a receipt — e.g. a selfie — is marked invalid with a reason, and excluded from the sum.
